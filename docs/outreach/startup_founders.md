# Startup founders to email

Companies from your startup sources (YC, a16z, Breakout List, Next Play, Ramp) that have at least one ML, data or AI role open to you right now. Ranked by the best fit score among their open roles. 65 companies.

How the names were found:
- Breakout List and YC companies: founders as listed by those sites. Names link to their X profiles where the Breakout List gives one.
- All others: from public news, company pages and funding announcements, checked on 6 October 2026.

Before you email anyone:
- **Emails are not included.** Do not guess them. Find each address with Apollo (use only "Verified" emails) or the company's own site, as in your cold email rules.
- **Check the name and role on LinkedIn first.** Leaders change. For example, Descript and Ambience Healthcare both changed CEO in 2025.
- **Big companies** (Cursor, Skydio, Descript, Komodo Health, Island, Thinking Machines Lab, Luma AI, Sourcegraph) rarely answer cold emails to the CEO. Apply on the role link first, then email a technical leader on the team.
- **Small companies** (under about 50 people) are the best ones to email the CEO or CTO directly.
- Mention the specific role and one project of yours that matches it, and keep it short.
