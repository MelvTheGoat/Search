## Cover letter

Ritual is building an AI-native blockchain and releases its work in the open, and this internship asks for someone who takes research from idea to prototype code. My strongest project already sits where AI and crypto meet.

web3-risk-mcp is an open-source MCP server that gives AI assistants six tools to check wallets, tokens and smart contracts on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum One, Polygon PoS and BNB Chain. It detects honeypots, rug pull signs and hidden owner powers, traces fund links to mixers and sanctioned wallets, and returns a 0 to 100 score with a reason for every point. I built an evaluation set of 34 hand-checked addresses with a record and replay harness, so claims about it can be tested.

On the research side, I take ideas to working code and test them honestly. I hand-implemented S-, T- and X-learners for an uplift study and validated them on synthetic data with a known effect before trusting real results. For fraud detection, I compared three deep sequence models with a strong baseline across 3 seeds.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, so statistical modelling is my strongest bonus area. I work in UTC+1, can start immediately and would be glad to talk.
