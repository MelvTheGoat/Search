## Cover letter

Matrix Global wants a customer-facing data scientist who builds models end to end, investigates anomalies in high-volume data, and trains users, with fraud detection, risk analytics or transaction monitoring as a plus. That mix fits my work closely.

My fraud detection system models customer transaction histories with three deep sequence models and a strong LightGBM baseline, reporting PR-AUC and precision at fixed review capacity across 3 seeds, with a breakdown by card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise. I handled extreme class imbalance, corrected the calibration, and served the model through FastAPI at 5.1 ms p99.

For the ML lifecycle, my churn project is tracked in MLflow and deployed on AWS ECS Fargate behind a load balancer, with CloudWatch logs and CI/CD on GitHub Actions. My reconciliation system finds duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies across multi-source transaction feeds.

I also teach machine learning to student cohorts and guide them through end-to-end projects on real datasets, so explaining technical work to non-specialists is part of my week. I have not used PySpark or Airflow. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and work in UTC+1. I would be glad to talk.
