## Cover letter

Your post asks for someone AI-savvy who understands both the value and the limits of AI-generated output. That is the line I have tried to hold in my own work, and it is why this role at Glovo stood out.

On the data side, my demand forecasting platform uses dbt and DuckDB for its transformations inside a Prefect pipeline, with automated tests that fail the build if any feature reads data from the future. In Reckon, my payment reconciliation system, I wrote the matching logic in DuckDB with temporal joins and tolerance thresholds to line up processor webhooks with bank settlement files.

On the AI side, I built a compliance assistant that does not trust the model blindly. It refuses when the evidence is weak and strips any citation the model gave but never retrieved. When one evaluation score looked poor, I traced it to the judge rather than the model and reported that plainly.

I use Git daily, teach data analysis with pandas and NumPy at SQI College of ICT, and hold a B.Sc. in Statistics. I have not used BigQuery or Looker yet, but SQL and dbt carry over. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Barcelona. I would be glad to talk.
