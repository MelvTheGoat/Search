## Cover letter

Lemrock helps brands sell inside conversational AI assistants, and wants an AI engineer to build agentic pipelines and improve the recommendation and ranking systems behind them, with evaluation rigour and tight latency limits. My work covers retrieval, ranking and causal measurement.

My compliance assistant combines BM25 and dense retrieval through reciprocal rank fusion and reached 0.96 recall@5 and 0.842 MRR on a 50-question labelled golden set. I evaluated retrieval and generation separately, and deployed it on GCP Cloud Run with Docker. My web3-risk-mcp server gives AI assistants six read-only tools with caching, rate limits and retries.

Your stack lists causal inference. In my uplift study on a randomised trial of 64,000 customers, I hand-implemented S, T and X learners and an EconML causal forest, and showed that a non-randomised list overstates the effect by 7.5×. For latency, I served a fraud model through ONNX Runtime at 5.1 ms p99 against a 50 ms budget.

I have not built a recommender system in production or fine-tuned an LLM. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics and the Claude Code In Action certificate, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Paris. I would be glad to talk.

Oluwatobi Melvyn Mayungbo
mlvyn.t@gmail.com

## Why this company

Lemrock is building how brands appear when people shop through AI assistants, a channel that is only now forming. Ranking and measuring what really works in that setting is a problem I would enjoy.
