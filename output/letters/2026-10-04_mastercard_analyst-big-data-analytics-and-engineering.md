## Cover letter

Mastercard's pre-sales team wants an analyst to build AI and analytics tools that quantify the value of its services, so internal teams can show customers what a service is worth. Putting a value on a decision is a large part of my work.

In my uplift modelling study on a randomised e-mail trial of 64,000 customers, I showed that the campaign works, with 1.25% conversion against 0.57%, and that uplift targeting was worth about $1,390 against about $3,170 for raising the budget. I wrote it up as a two-page decision memo for a marketing director, because a result only matters if the business can act on it.

My demand forecasting platform models business cost directly, penalising unmet demand at 3× idle supply, which cut expected cost by 25% on an identical model. My payment reconciliation system finds uncollected revenue, duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies across transaction feeds.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics and the Google Data Analytics and Advanced Data Analytics certificates, and I work in SQL and Python. I have not used Tableau or Power BI. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Pune. I would be glad to talk.

Oluwatobi Melvyn Mayungbo
mlvyn.t@gmail.com

## Why this company

Mastercard's services are easier to sell when their value is measured honestly. Turning data into a clear number that a business can act on is the work I enjoy most.
