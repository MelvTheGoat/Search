## Cover letter

Lendable's AI team has built a customer chatbot that completes account actions end to end and AI document parsers, and wants someone to improve them through better prompts, retrieval pipelines, agent workflows and evaluations. Those are the parts of AI work I have built myself.

My compliance assistant combines BM25 and dense retrieval through reciprocal rank fusion and reached 0.96 recall@5 and 0.842 MRR on a 50-question labelled golden set. It refuses rather than guesses, using a sentinel token enforced across the prompt, the output guardrail and the evaluation harness, and it strips any citation the model gave but never retrieved. I scored retrieval and generation separately, so a failure could be traced to one component.

I also built web3-risk-mcp, an MCP server that gives AI assistants six read-only tools with caching, rate limits and retries, and a credit decisioning system with calibrated default probabilities and reason codes, so I know the lending side of the problem too.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, ship services with FastAPI and Docker, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to London. I would be glad to talk.
