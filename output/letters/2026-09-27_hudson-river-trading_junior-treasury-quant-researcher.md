## Cover letter

HRT's PostTrade team is building optimisation models for funding efficiency and collateral allocation, and this junior role puts research straight into production systems. I am drawn to problems where a model's output is a decision with a clear cost attached.

In my demand forecasting platform, unmet demand costs 3× as much as idle supply, so I did not ship the median forecast. I shipped the quantile that minimises expected cost, which cut expected cost by 25% on the same model. In my credit decisioning system, I set approve and decline cutoffs to minimise expected loss, which is why I chose the model on calibration rather than ranking. In my uplift study, I compared the value of better targeting, about $1,390, with the value of a bigger budget, about $3,170, so the decision became an allocation question.

I work in Python with NumPy, pandas and PyTorch, and validate with backtesting across 6 folds. I also built a double-entry ledger API where every transaction must sum to zero, which taught me how money moves between accounts.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics. I have not worked in treasury or prime brokerage and would learn it quickly. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to New York. I would be glad to talk.
