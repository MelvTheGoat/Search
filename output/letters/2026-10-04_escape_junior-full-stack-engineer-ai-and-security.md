## Cover letter

Escape builds AI agents that find and exploit vulnerabilities in applications and APIs, and wants a junior full-stack engineer to work across the product and turn AI research into real features. Security-minded tooling for AI is what my recent projects are about.

My web3-risk-mcp server gives AI assistants six read-only tools to check a crypto wallet, token or contract for risk before anyone interacts with it. It never asks for a private key, never signs transactions, and its RPC client refuses any method outside a read-only allow-list. It detects honeypot tokens, hidden owner powers and fund links to exploiters and phishing addresses, and its HTTP layer handles caching, rate limits and retries, so one failing source never breaks a check.

On the full-stack side, my reconciliation system has a FastAPI and DuckDB backend with a React review screen, and my double-entry ledger API runs on PostgreSQL with 138 tests against a real database.

I have not worked with TypeScript, Svelte or GraphQL yet, and I am ready to learn them. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Paris. I would be glad to talk.
