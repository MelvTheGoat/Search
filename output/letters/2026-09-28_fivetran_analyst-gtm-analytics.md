## Cover letter

Fivetran's GTM Analytics team answers questions from Sales, Marketing and RevOps by writing SQL, checking data against source systems, and turning results into plain-language recommendations, using Claude along the way. That is how I already work.

My clearest example is an uplift study on the Hillstrom e-mail trial with 64,000 customers, delivered as a two-page decision memo for a marketing director. It showed the campaign works, with 1.25% conversion against 0.57%, but that a non-randomised list overstates the effect by 7.5×. Better targeting was worth about $1,390 against about $3,170 for a bigger budget. The memo gave a recommendation, not just numbers.

I write SQL daily and have built transformations with dbt and DuckDB, and I checked data against its source as an internal auditor, where I looked for inconsistencies in financial records. I use Claude Code every day to write and debug code, and I review everything it produces before I trust it.

I also teach data analysis to student cohorts, so explaining results to people who are not technical is part of my job. I can start immediately, am willing to relocate to Costa Rica and would be glad to talk.
