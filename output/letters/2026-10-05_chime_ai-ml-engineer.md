## Cover letter

Chime's AI/ML Engineers own problems end to end, from framing the question to a model in production that moves a metric, across Trust and Safety, Lending and Growth. My strongest work sits in exactly those areas.

For fraud, I built three deep sequence models (GRU, TCN and a causally-masked Transformer) over customer transaction histories and benchmarked them against a strong LightGBM baseline across 3 seeds, with a breakdown by card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise. Under an explicit cost model, the sequence models cut expected cost per transaction by 27%. I handled extreme class imbalance, corrected calibration from an ECE of 0.0063 to 0.0005, and served the model through ONNX Runtime at 5.1 ms p99 against a 50 ms budget.

For lending, my credit decisioning system produces calibrated default probabilities, cost-optimal cutoffs and adverse action reason codes, with a fairness audit across sex, age, education and marital status. For growth, my uplift study on a randomised trial of 64,000 customers showed which customers a campaign actually helps.

I have not used Spark, Airflow, Kafka or Snowflake. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate. I would be glad to talk.
