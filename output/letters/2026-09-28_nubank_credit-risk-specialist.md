## Cover letter

Your post says the job is to make the pipeline behind the ECL calculation reliable, automated and self-monitoring, so each cycle runs low-touch and reproducible. That is the kind of system I like building most.

My demand forecasting platform runs as a Prefect pipeline covering ingestion, dbt and DuckDB transformations, backtesting, drift monitoring with Evidently and conditional retraining, with experiments tracked in MLflow. Automated leakage tests fail the build if any feature reads past the forecast origin, so problems surface before they reach the numbers. It is validated with rolling-origin backtesting across 6 folds.

On the credit side, I built a decisioning system that produces calibrated default probabilities, cost-optimal cutoffs and adverse action reason codes, with an append-only audit trail that lets any decision be rebuilt months later. I compared gradient boosting with a WOE-binned logistic scorecard and put calibration first, because expected loss needs true probabilities. That audit trail is the same idea as capturing control evidence from source systems instead of by hand.

I write Python and SQL every day and hold a B.Sc. in Statistics. I can start immediately, am willing to relocate to São Paulo and would be glad to talk.
