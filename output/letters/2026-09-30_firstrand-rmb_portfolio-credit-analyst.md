## Cover letter

RMB's credit team wants a Portfolio Credit Analyst to monitor exposures against risk appetite, run stress and scenario analysis, manage the ECL model and prepare clear reports for committees and regulators. My credit project and my audit work are close to this.

I built a credit decisioning system that produces calibrated default probabilities and expected-loss based cutoffs, with reason codes and an append-only audit trail so any decision can be reconstructed months later. I judged models on calibration, using Brier score, reliability diagrams and expected calibration error, because expected loss depends on true probabilities. That is the same concern behind a sound ECL figure.

For scenario work, my forecasting platform validates with rolling-origin backtesting across 6 folds and weighs outcomes under an explicit cost model. I also built a retrieval assistant over banking regulations that answers with citations to the exact clause.

As an internal auditor, I reviewed financial and operational records to find inconsistencies, so I am used to checking figures before they reach a report. I teach machine learning, so I can explain results to non-specialists. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics and can start immediately. I would be glad to talk.

RMB's credit portfolio spans corporate lending, markets and treasury, so portfolio analysis there shows how credit risk works across a whole bank. The role combines analytics with governance, which suits my statistics and audit background.
