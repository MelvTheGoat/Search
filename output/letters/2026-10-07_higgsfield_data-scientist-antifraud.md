## Cover letter

Higgsfield wants someone to own account-abuse scoring end to end, telling organised operators apart from honest heavy users before the compute is burned, and to ship scores with reasons, never scores alone. That is how I build fraud systems.

My fraud detection system models customer transaction histories and reports precision at fixed review capacity in the top 0.1%, 0.5% and 1%, with a breakdown by card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise. Under an explicit cost model, the sequence models cut expected cost per transaction by 27%, and I corrected calibration from an ECE of 0.0063 to 0.0005, so a threshold means what it says.

For linkage, my web3-risk-mcp scorer traces fund links to mixers, exploiters and phishing addresses over 1 or 2 hops. It is fully explainable: every finding has a stable ID, a public rule table turns findings into points, and missing data lowers confidence instead of the score. I measured it on 34 hand-checked addresses, and my reconciliation system sends uncertain cases to a review queue with audit trails.

I have not used BigQuery. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, work in SQL and Python, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Almaty. I would be glad to talk.
