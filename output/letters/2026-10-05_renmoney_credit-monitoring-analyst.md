## Cover letter

Renmoney's Credit Monitoring Analyst watches loan portfolios after disbursement, spots early warning signs of problem loans and makes sure every facility stays within its approved terms. My credit and audit work fit this closely.

I built a credit decisioning system that produces calibrated default probabilities, cost-optimal approve and decline cutoffs, adverse action reason codes and an append-only audit trail, so any decision can be reconstructed months later. I compared gradient boosting with a traditional WOE-binned logistic scorecard and chose calibration quality over ranking metrics, because expected loss needs true probabilities.

As an internal auditor, I analysed financial and operational records to find inconsistencies and irregularities, checking complex transaction data by hand. My payment reconciliation system does the same in software, finding uncollected revenue, duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies, and sending exceptions to a review screen.

Daily reports such as past due obligations and imminent maturities need careful, accurate data work, which I do in SQL and Python. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics and can start immediately. I would be glad to talk.

## Why this company

Renmoney lends to people and small businesses that banks often miss, so catching early warning signs protects both the customer and the loan book. Credit risk is the area I have built most of my work around.
