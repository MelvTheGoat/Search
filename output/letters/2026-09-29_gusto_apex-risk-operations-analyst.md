## Cover letter

Gusto's APEX team protects embedded payroll partners by spotting uncharacteristic behaviour in partner activity, and its analysts act as the human in the loop for AI-powered fraud workflows. Finding patterns in payment data and checking what a model flags is the work I know best.

My fraud detection system models customer transaction histories and breaks results down by card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise, because each attack looks different in the data. I measured precision in the top 0.1%, 0.5% and 1% of transactions, which is how a review team with limited time decides which cases to open first.

I also built a payment reconciliation system that detects duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies, with a review screen showing match confidence, audit trails and suggested actions. That is the same idea as validating AI outputs: the system proposes, and a person makes the final call. My web3-risk-mcp tool gives a reason for every point of its risk score, so every decision can be explained.

As an internal auditor, I reviewed financial records to find irregularities. I use Claude Code every day and check its work before I trust it. I can start immediately, am willing to relocate to Denver and would be glad to talk.

Gusto is moving risk operations to an AI-first model, and this team decides how people and AI share fraud decisions for payroll partners. Payroll fraud harms small businesses directly, so getting those decisions right matters.
