## Cover letter

Wheely's Data Science teams work on ETA prediction, dynamic pricing, matching and supply, and own each model from research to production. My closest project is a demand forecast for city transport.

I built an hourly demand forecasting system for NYC taxi zones that produces 24-hour quantile forecasts through a Prefect pipeline covering ingestion, dbt and DuckDB transformations, backtesting, drift monitoring and conditional retraining. I penalised unmet demand at 3× idle supply and shipped the cost-optimal quantile instead of the median, which cut expected cost by 25% on the same model and beat a seasonal-naive baseline by 32%. Rolling-origin backtesting across 6 folds and automated leakage tests kept the results honest.

For matching and optimisation, my FPL AI Manager rebuilds the best legal squad each gameweek under budget, formation and transfer rules. For latency-sensitive serving, my fraud model runs through ONNX Runtime at 5.1 ms p99 against a 50 ms budget.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics with a strong grounding in probability. The post asks for more experience than I have, so I am applying on the strength of this work. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to London. I would be glad to talk.
