## Cover letter

Flutterwave's Risk team is moving from reactive analytics to predictive models that detect fraud, assess risk and protect the payment infrastructure. That shift is exactly what my fraud detection project was built to study.

I built sequence models, a GRU, a temporal convolutional network and a causally masked Transformer, over customer transaction histories, and benchmarked them against a LightGBM baseline on velocity and deviation features. I measured precision at fixed review capacity and broke results down by card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise. Under an explicit cost model, the sequence models cut expected cost per transaction by 27%. The model is served through ONNX Runtime at 5.1 ms p99 with per-decision audit logs, which supports the documentation for audits your post asks for.

For monitoring, my forecasting platform tracks drift with Evidently and retrains when needed. For payments data, Reckon reconciles processor webhooks with bank settlement files, and my compliance assistant answers questions over central bank circulars.

I can start immediately and work onsite with your team. I would be glad to talk.
