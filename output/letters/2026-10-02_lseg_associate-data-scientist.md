## Cover letter

LSEG's Entity Content team wants a data scientist to automate how entity data is loaded, find patterns in legal entity content and use machine learning to improve core processes. My projects combine these three things: data pipelines, analysis and models people can trust.

My reconciliation system matches multi-source transaction feeds with DuckDB and FastAPI, using temporal join logic and tolerance thresholds, and a review screen shows match confidence scores and suggested actions so people only check the exceptions. That is the same shape of problem as matching entity records from different sources.

I work in Python with scikit-learn, pandas and NumPy. My credit decisioning system compares a WOE-binned logistic scorecard with gradient boosting and audits fairness across customer groups. My uplift study on 64,000 customers was delivered as a two-page decision memo for a marketing director, and I teach machine learning, so explaining results to non-technical people is part of my work.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Gdynia. I would be glad to talk.
