## Cover letter

Brex's Fraud Operations team manages transaction fraud, first-party fraud and account takeovers, makes risk-based calls on high-value transactions and helps improve the AI tools behind those calls. My fraud project and my audit work are close to this.

My sequence-based fraud detection system models customer transaction histories and breaks results down by card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise. I measured precision in the top 0.1%, 0.5% and 1% of transactions, which is how a team with limited review capacity decides what to look at first, and the sequence models cut expected cost per transaction by 27% under an explicit cost model.

I also built an explainable risk scorer that gives a reason for every point, and a reconciliation system with a review screen showing audit trails and suggested actions. As an internal auditor, I checked complex transaction data by hand to find irregularities.

The post asks for more experience than I have, so I am applying on the strength of this work. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, write SQL and Python, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Salt Lake City. I would be glad to talk.
