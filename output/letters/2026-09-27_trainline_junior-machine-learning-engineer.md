## Cover letter

Trainline's ML teams power search, recommendations, pricing and routing, and customer support agents, and own each model from idea to production. As a junior, I want to learn inside a team that works end to end like that.

The closest project to your work is my demand forecasting platform. It predicts hourly taxi demand across NYC zones for the next 24 hours through a Prefect pipeline covering ingestion, dbt and DuckDB transformations, backtesting, drift monitoring and retraining. Because unmet demand costs more than idle supply, I shipped the cost-optimal quantile instead of the median, which cut expected cost by 25% and beat a seasonal-naive baseline by 32%. Transport demand is a problem I have already spent time inside.

I have also built a churn model with XGBoost, tracked in MLflow and deployed with Docker on AWS ECS Fargate with GitHub Actions CI/CD, and a retrieval assistant that combines BM25 and dense embeddings, which is close to where search ranking starts.

I teach machine learning with PyTorch, scikit-learn, pandas and NumPy at SQI College of ICT, so I am used to explaining models clearly. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to London. I would be glad to talk.
