## Cover letter

Lula funds small and medium businesses, and this role supports credit modelling, credit risk strategy and credit insights, from scorecards and affordability to pricing and profit models. Credit decisioning is the area I have built most deeply in.

I built a credit decisioning system that produces calibrated default probabilities, sets approve and decline cutoffs that minimise expected cost, gives adverse action reason codes and keeps an append-only audit trail so any decision can be rebuilt later. I compared gradient boosting with a traditional WOE-binned logistic scorecard and chose on calibration, using Brier score and reliability diagrams, because pricing and profit need true probabilities, not just a ranking. I also audited fairness across sex, age, education and marital status and presented the trade-off as a policy decision.

For strategy and test frameworks, my uplift study on 64,000 customers compares targeting policies under a fixed budget with Qini curves and bootstrap intervals, and my forecasting platform ships the decision that minimises business cost.

My stack matches yours in Python, SQL, dbt and GitHub. I have not used Snowflake or Hex yet. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Cape Town. I would be glad to talk.
