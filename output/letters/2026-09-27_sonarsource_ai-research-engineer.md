## Cover letter

Sonar's Data and Agentic team applies LLM and agent techniques to source code, and the role asks for high-quality datasets, clear success metrics and honest benchmarks. Benchmarking properly is the habit I have tried hardest to build.

For my compliance assistant, I built hybrid retrieval, BM25 and dense embeddings fused with reciprocal rank fusion, reaching 0.96 recall@5 on a 50-question labelled set. I tested my section-aware chunking against a fixed-window baseline instead of assuming it was better, and when a generation score came out at 0.40, I traced it to the judge and reported that plainly.

For web3-risk-mcp, an MCP server that gives AI assistants six risk tools, I built a record and replay harness over 34 hand-checked addresses. In fraud detection, I compared three deep sequence models with a strong LightGBM baseline across 3 seeds, so no single run could decide the answer.

I have built projects with AI coding tools and hold the Claude Code in Action certificate, so code quality in the age of agents is a problem I see up close. I have not fine-tuned with LoRA yet. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Geneva. I would be glad to talk.
