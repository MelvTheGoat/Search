## Cover letter

IMC's graduate researchers in digital assets test trading ideas on data from crypto exchanges and DeFi protocols and help turn them into production strategies. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics and have built tools that analyse on-chain data.

My web3-risk-mcp tool reads live data across Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum One, Polygon PoS and BNB Chain to detect honeypot tokens, holder concentration, unlocked liquidity and fund links to mixers and exploiters. It pulls from several sources at once, so I built caching, rate limits and retries, and missing data lowers confidence rather than the score.

For testing ideas honestly, my forecasting platform uses rolling-origin backtesting across 6 folds with automated leakage tests that fail the build if any feature reads past the forecast origin. It beat a seasonal-naive baseline by 32%. In my fraud work I compared sequence models over transaction histories across 3 seeds rather than trusting one run.

I also ran a causal study with bootstrap intervals and reported a failed placebo test openly, because a result you cannot trust is worse than none. I program in Python, can start immediately, am willing to relocate to Zug and would be glad to talk.
