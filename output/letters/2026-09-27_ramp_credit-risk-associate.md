## Cover letter

Ramp wants credit risk people who build the first useful version of a tool instead of writing a recommendation for someone else, and who can show how they checked an AI tool's output. That is how I work, and credit decisioning is the project I know best.

I built a credit decisioning system from scratch. It produces calibrated default probabilities, sets approve and decline cutoffs that minimise expected cost, gives adverse action reason codes and keeps an append-only audit trail so any decision can be rebuilt months later. I chose between gradient boosting and a WOE logistic scorecard on calibration, because limits and pricing need true probabilities. I also audited fairness across sex, age, education and marital status and presented the tradeoff as a policy decision.

On the build side, I made a human review screen for my payment reconciliation system, with match confidence and suggested actions, close to the case review and QA loops in your post.

I build with AI coding tools and hold the Claude Code in Action certificate. A recent failure mode I caught: an evaluation judge scored my assistant's refusals at 0.40, and I traced the problem to the judge, not the model, before trusting the number.

I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to New York. I would be glad to talk.
