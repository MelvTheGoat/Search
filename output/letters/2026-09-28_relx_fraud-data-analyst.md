## Cover letter

LexisNexis Risk Solutions wants a Fraud Data Analyst who analyses fraud patterns, builds and tunes detection rules and models, and explains the results to bank and e-commerce clients. Fraud detection is the focus of my strongest work.

My fraud detection system benchmarks sequence models against a LightGBM baseline on velocity and deviation features, with results broken down by card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise. I measured precision in the top 0.1%, 0.5% and 1% of transactions, because clients care about what their review team can actually handle. Under an explicit cost model, the sequence models cut expected cost per transaction by 27%.

For rules, I built web3-risk-mcp, a fully explainable scorer where every finding has a stable ID and a reason, so anyone can see why a case was flagged. My credit decisioning system also gives reason codes for every decision.

As an internal auditor, I reviewed financial and operational records to find inconsistencies and irregularities. I now teach machine learning to student cohorts, so explaining technical findings to non-specialists is part of my job. I work in Python and SQL, can start immediately and would be glad to talk.
