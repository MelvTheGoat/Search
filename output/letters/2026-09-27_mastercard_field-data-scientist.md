## Cover letter

Mastercard Identity's field data scientists run proof of value tests with clients and help their data scientists get the most from Identity's data in fraud models. I build fraud models and explain them to people, and this role asks for both.

My fraud detection system compares sequence models with a LightGBM baseline and reports precision in the top 0.1%, 0.5% and 1% of scored transactions, which is how a client judges whether a new signal is worth buying. I broke results down by attack type, including account takeover, and cut expected cost per transaction by 27% under an explicit cost model.

For explaining signals, my credit decisioning system gives adverse action reason codes for every decision and includes a fairness audit, and my web3-risk-mcp tool gives a reason for every point of its risk score. Clear reasons make a proof of value easier to trust.

I teach machine learning to student cohorts, so presenting technical results to mixed audiences is part of my job, and I work in Python and SQL. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Toronto. I would be glad to talk.

Oluwatobi Melvyn Mayungbo
mlvyn.t@gmail.com

## Why this company

Identity verification sits at the start of every fraud decision, and Mastercard's data covers a large share of the world's payments. The role mixes model work with client conversations, which suits someone who likes building models and explaining them.
