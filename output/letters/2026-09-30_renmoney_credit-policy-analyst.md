## Cover letter

Renmoney wants a Credit Policy Analyst to design eligibility rules, credit limits and scorecards for new products such as credit cards, credit lines and BNPL, and to test policy changes before they go live. Building credit decision rules and testing their impact is what my credit project does.

My credit decisioning system produces calibrated default probabilities and cost-optimal approve and decline cutoffs, with adverse action reason codes and an append-only audit trail. I compared a WOE-binned logistic scorecard with gradient boosting and chose on calibration, because expected loss needs true probabilities. I also audited fairness across sex, age, education and marital status, and presented the accuracy and fairness trade-off as a policy decision rather than hiding it.

Policy simulation is familiar too. In my forecasting work I chose the forecast under an explicit cost model and cut expected cost by 25% on the same model, and my uplift study compared better targeting with a bigger budget before recommending one.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics and work in SQL and Python. The post asks for more experience than I have, so I am applying on the strength of this work. I can start immediately and would be glad to talk.
