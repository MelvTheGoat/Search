## Cover letter

Netcraft takes down around a third of the world's phishing attacks, and its Novel Classification team works against attackers who change tactics to evade it. Adversarial detection is the theme of most of my projects.

My web3-risk-mcp server checks crypto wallets, tokens and contracts before anyone interacts with them. It flags honeypots, rug pull signs and fund links to mixers, sanctioned wallets and phishing addresses, and gives a reason for every point of its score. I tested it on 34 hand-checked addresses, reporting ROC AUC, precision and recall. Its HTTP layer caches, rate-limits and retries with backoff, so one failing source never breaks an investigation.

My fraud detection system compares sequence models with a LightGBM baseline on precision at fixed review capacity, across card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise. I handled extreme class imbalance while keeping probabilities calibrated, and served the model at 5.1 ms p99 with ONNX Runtime and FastAPI.

I have not written Go or Perl yet and am happy to learn both. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to London, Manchester or Bath. I would be glad to talk.
