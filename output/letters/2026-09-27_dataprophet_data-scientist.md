## Cover letter

DataProphet puts AI on the factory floor and wants data scientists who build models for real operational use, not only for notebooks. That is the standard I have held my own projects to.

My demand forecasting platform predicts hourly demand for the next 24 hours through an orchestrated pipeline with backtesting, drift monitoring and conditional retraining. I validated it with rolling-origin backtesting across 6 folds and leakage tests that fail the build. Because shortfalls cost more than surplus, I shipped the quantile that minimises expected cost rather than the median, cutting expected cost by 25%. Acting ahead of time on a cost model is close to the pre-emptive approach your post describes.

My fraud detection work uses deep sequence models over event histories, compared against a strong LightGBM baseline across 3 seeds and served at 5.1 ms p99. For experiments, I built a power and MDE calculator and a CUPED demo alongside a causal uplift study.

I teach machine learning at SQI College of ICT, so explaining models to non-technical people is part of my job. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Cape Town. I would be glad to talk.
