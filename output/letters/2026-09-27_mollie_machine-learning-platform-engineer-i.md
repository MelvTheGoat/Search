## Cover letter

Mollie's ML Platform team deploys models for Risk and Fraud, Payments and more, and keeps them healthy in production. That path from a trained model to something people can depend on is the part of machine learning I enjoy most.

My demand forecasting platform is a small version of that job. A Prefect pipeline handles ingestion, dbt and DuckDB transformations, backtesting, drift monitoring with Evidently and conditional retraining, with runs tracked in MLflow and CI on GitHub Actions. For my fraud model, I exported to ONNX Runtime, which was 2.9× faster than eager PyTorch at batch size 1, and served it through FastAPI at 5.1 ms p99 against a 50 ms budget. I have deployed services with Docker on GCP Cloud Run, AWS ECS Fargate and Railway, and I work with scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy, XGBoost, LightGBM and MLflow.

I have not yet used Terraform or Kubernetes in a project, and I would be keen to learn them on your platform. I also built a RAG assistant, so hosting GenAI tooling like LiteLLM interests me.

I can start immediately and would relocate to Lisbon. I would be glad to talk.
