## Cover letter

Kuda's Decision Science team supports credit from acquisition to collections, with score cut-off analysis, rule-based decision engines, affordability checks and A/B tests. Credit decisioning and experiment design are the two areas my strongest projects cover.

I built a credit decisioning system that produces calibrated default probabilities and cost-optimal approve and decline cutoffs, with reason codes for every decision and an audit trail that lets any decision be rebuilt later. I compared gradient boosting with a WOE-binned logistic scorecard and put calibration first, because expected loss needs true probabilities rather than rankings. I also audited fairness across customer groups and showed the trade-off as a policy choice.

For testing lending and collections changes, I ran an uplift study on 64,000 customers to decide who to contact under a fixed budget, evaluated with Qini curves and bootstrap intervals, and built a power and MDE calculator with a CUPED demo so tests reach an answer sooner.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics and work in SQL and Python every day. The post asks for more years than I have, so I am applying on the strength of this work. I can start immediately and would be glad to talk.
