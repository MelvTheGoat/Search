## Cover letter

Harmonic Security wants an AI/ML Product Engineer to turn raw data on how people and agents use AI into insights customers can trust, with evaluation frameworks and ground-truth sets that prove an insight holds before anyone sees it. Being strict about validity runs through my work.

My fraud detection system is a set of production-style classifiers over customer transaction histories. I reported PR-AUC and precision at fixed review capacity across 3 seeds against a strong LightGBM baseline, with a breakdown by attack type, and I served it through FastAPI at 5.1 ms p99. My web3-risk-mcp scorer gives a reason for every point and a confidence level based on how many sources answered, and I measured it on 34 hand-checked addresses with ROC AUC, precision and recall.

I also check automated results with people. My reconciliation system sends low-confidence matches to a review screen with audit trails, and my retrieval assistant was scored against a 50-question labelled golden set.

My production code is Python, tested and deployed with Docker and CI. I have not used Java or TypeScript. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to London. I would be glad to talk.
