## Cover letter

DoorDash's Ads delivery team builds the models behind pacing, bidding and targeting, and measures them with lift tests and budget experiments. Spending a fixed budget where it causes the most extra value is the question my uplift project was built around.

On the Hillstrom trial of 64,000 customers, I chose who to contact under a fixed budget using S-, T- and X-learners and a causal forest, evaluated with Qini curves, AUUC and bootstrap intervals. I found that a response model mails five times as many customers the campaign harms as an uplift model does, and that better targeting was worth about $1,390 against about $3,170 for a bigger budget. I added a power and MDE calculator and a CUPED variance reduction demo for planning experiments.

For forecasting, my demand platform predicts hourly NYC taxi demand and ships the cost-optimal quantile rather than the median, which cut expected cost by 25%. For serving, my fraud model runs at 5.1 ms p99 through ONNX Runtime.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics and the Claude Code in Action certificate. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to San Francisco or Sunnyvale. I would be glad to talk.
