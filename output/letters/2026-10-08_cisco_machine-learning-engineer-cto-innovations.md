## Cover letter

Cisco's CTO organisation is building AI applications that turn networking and security telemetry into operational intelligence, with ETL pipelines and production LLM, RAG and ML systems for troubleshooting, anomaly detection and incident analysis. I have built each of those pieces.

My compliance assistant combines BM25 and dense retrieval through reciprocal rank fusion and reached 0.96 recall@5 and 0.842 MRR on a 50-question labelled golden set. I evaluated retrieval and generation separately, added refusal guardrails, and deployed it on GCP Cloud Run.

For pipelines, my forecasting platform runs as an orchestrated Prefect pipeline covering ingestion, dbt and DuckDB transformations, backtesting and drift monitoring with Evidently. My reconciliation pipeline uses DuckDB and FastAPI with temporal joins to match asynchronous event feeds, and my fraud system finds anomalies in transaction sequences and serves at 5.1 ms p99.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, completed in 2025, and my experience comes from projects. I have not used Spark, Airflow, Snowflake or LangChain. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to San Jose. I would be glad to talk.
