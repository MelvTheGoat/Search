## Cover letter

Databricks wants agents like Genie Code to author, train, deploy and monitor classical ML, such as fraud detection, forecasting and pricing, at expert level. I have built those classical systems by hand, and I have built tools for AI agents, so I know both sides of what the agent has to do.

On forecasting, my demand platform predicts hourly taxi demand across NYC zones, validated with rolling-origin backtesting across 6 folds and leakage tests. I shipped the quantile that minimises business cost rather than the median, which cut expected cost by 25%. On fraud, I compared sequence models with a LightGBM baseline by attack type, cut expected cost per transaction by 27%, and served the model at 5.1 ms p99.

On the agent side, web3-risk-mcp gives AI assistants six tools to investigate crypto risk, with an explainable score and an HTTP layer that survives failing sources. The monitoring and self-healing your post describes is close to my forecasting pipeline, which watches drift with Evidently and retrains when needed.

Your post welcomes people who are keen to grow, and that describes me. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Belgrade. I would be glad to talk.
