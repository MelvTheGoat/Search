## Cover letter

Mercor's Research Engineers own the benchmarks, evaluation systems and failure analysis that inform how frontier models are trained, including how tool use and agentic behaviour are measured. Evaluation and failure analysis are the parts of AI work I do most carefully.

In my compliance assistant, I built an evaluation harness that scores retrieval and generation separately against a 50-question labelled golden set, so a failure can be traced to one component. When the offline judge gave a 0.40 refusal-correctness result, I traced it to a lexical-overlap limit in the judge itself rather than reporting it as model behaviour, and named re-validation against a live provider as the next step.

For tool use, my web3-risk-mcp server gives AI assistants six read-only tools, and I measured it with a record and replay harness on 34 hand-checked addresses, run twice to show what the signals catch with and without a local list. In my fraud work, I benchmarked three deep sequence models against a strong LightGBM baseline across 3 seeds and isolated the separate failure modes of two imbalance methods.

I have not worked on a post-training team. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to London. I would be glad to talk.
