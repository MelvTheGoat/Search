## Cover letter

Tripledot's Puzzleplay team wants an analyst to own the fraud side of its user segmentation: refining signals such as IP spoofing, VPN use and unusual play patterns, monitoring how the segments perform and improving manual review. Designing and checking fraud signals is the core of my fraud work.

My web3-risk-mcp tool is a rule-based, fully explainable scorer: tools produce findings with stable IDs, a public rule table turns them into points, and decisive findings set a floor of 75 so trust signals can never hide them. I measured it on 34 hand-checked addresses, and ran it twice to show what the signals catch without the local list of known bad addresses.

My sequence-based fraud detection system breaks results down by attack type, because each fraud pattern looks different in the data, and measures precision at fixed review capacity, which decides how big a manual review queue can be.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, work in SQL and Python, and can start immediately. I would be glad to talk.

Puzzleplay pays out real prizes, so fraud directly affects revenue share and fairness for honest players. Owning fraud signals end to end, as patterns change, is the kind of problem I enjoy.
