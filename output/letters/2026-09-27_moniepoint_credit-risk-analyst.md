## Cover letter

This role is the embedded credit risk owner in a consumer lending squad, covering credit policies and decision rules, scorecards with Decision Science, portfolio monitoring, and compliance with CBN and KYC/AML standards. Risk by design is how I built my credit project.

My credit decisioning system produces calibrated default probabilities, sets approve and decline cutoffs from an explicit cost model, gives adverse action reason codes and keeps an append-only audit trail so any decision can be traced months later. I compared gradient boosting with a WOE logistic scorecard on calibration, and audited fairness across sex, age, education and marital status, presenting the trade-off as a policy choice.

On regulation, I built a compliance assistant that answers questions over CBN circulars and the NDPA, with every citation checked against the clause it came from.

For portfolio work, my forecasting platform tracks drift and retrains, and my uplift study compares targeting policies under a fixed budget, similar to limit and pricing questions.

I work in SQL and Python with NumPy and pandas. I can start immediately and work onsite with your team. I would be glad to talk.
