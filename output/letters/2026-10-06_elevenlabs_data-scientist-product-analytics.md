## Cover letter

ElevenLabs wants a product data scientist to own activation, adoption and retention metrics, and to design and read out product experiments such as A/B tests and onboarding changes. Experiments are where my strongest work is.

In my uplift study on a randomised e-mail trial of 64,000 customers, I showed the campaign works, with 1.25% conversion against 0.57%, but that a non-randomised list overstates the effect by 7.5×, and that a response model mails five times as many customers the campaign harms as an uplift model does. I evaluated it with Qini curves, decile tables and bootstrap intervals, and reported a failed placebo test openly instead of hiding it.

I also built a power and MDE calculator and a CUPED variance-reduction demo, the tools that decide how long a test must run before its result means anything. For pipelines, my forecasting platform transforms data with dbt and DuckDB, with automated tests that fail the build on leakage.

I work in SQL and Python, and I would push back on a metric that does not answer the question. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and work in UTC+1. I would be glad to talk.
