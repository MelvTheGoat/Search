## Cover letter

Lendable's chatbot already works across loans, credit cards and motor finance and completes account actions on its own. Holding it to the standard of your best operations agents is the right bar, and evaluation is where I have put most of my LLM work.

I built a compliance assistant over central bank circulars and data protection law. It combines BM25 and dense retrieval with reciprocal rank fusion and reaches 0.96 recall@5 on a 50-question labelled set. I evaluate retrieval and generation separately so a failure points to one component, enforce a refusal rule across the prompt, output guardrail and evaluation harness, and strip any citation the model gives that was never retrieved. When one score came out at 0.40, I traced it to the judge, not the model, and said so instead of overstating the result.

I have also built an MCP server that gives AI assistants six tools to check crypto wallets and tokens for risk with an explainable score, and a credit decisioning system with reason codes and an audit trail, so I know the lending side too.

I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to London. I would be glad to talk.
