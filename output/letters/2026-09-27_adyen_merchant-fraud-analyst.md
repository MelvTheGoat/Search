## Cover letter

Adyen's Merchant Fraud team watches transaction patterns and account behaviour in real time, and handles merchant and shopper fraud as it happens. Merchant fraud is one of the attack types I studied most closely in my own work.

My fraud detection system looks at each customer's transaction history as a sequence, and I broke results down by attack type: card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise. Each leaves a different pattern, so I measured precision in the top 0.1%, 0.5% and 1% of scored transactions, the share a team can realistically review. The sequence models cut expected cost per transaction by 27% under an explicit cost model.

For scams, my web3-risk-mcp tool detects honeypot tokens, rug pull signs, hidden owner powers and fund links to mixers, sanctioned wallets and phishing addresses. Reckon, my reconciliation system, flags duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies for a human to review.

I analysed financial records for irregularities as an internal auditor. I have not yet worked on a fraud team at a financial institution, and I am ready to learn recall handling and bank coordination quickly. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Amsterdam. I would be glad to talk.
