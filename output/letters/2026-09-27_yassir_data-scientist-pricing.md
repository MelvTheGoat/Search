## Cover letter

Yassir's AI team is working on dynamic and surge pricing, discount and commission optimisation, and personalised pricing across a super app built for Africans on the continent. Pricing under real costs is the problem my strongest projects deal with.

My demand forecasting platform predicts hourly ride demand across NYC taxi zones for the next 24 hours, close to the demand signal behind surge pricing and dispatch. Because unmet demand costs more than idle supply, I shipped the cost-optimal quantile instead of the median, which cut expected cost by 25% and beat a seasonal-naive baseline by 32%. It runs as a Prefect pipeline with backtesting, drift monitoring and conditional retraining.

For discounts and personalisation, I ran an uplift study on 64,000 customers to decide who to target under a fixed budget, using S-, T- and X-learners and a causal forest. Better targeting was worth about $1,390 against about $3,170 for a bigger budget, which is the trade-off behind discount spend.

I write production Python and SQL and deploy with FastAPI and Docker. My degree is a B.Sc. in Statistics rather than a Master's. I work in UTC+1, can start immediately and would be glad to talk.
