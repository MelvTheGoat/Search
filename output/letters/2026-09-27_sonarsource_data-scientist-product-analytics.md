## Cover letter

Sonar checks the code that AI assistants and agents write, and your Data and Insights team wants to link how customers use it to expansion, churn risk and account health. Tying behaviour to outcomes, and being honest about what is causal, is the part of data science I care about most.

I built a telco churn model end to end: feature engineering, an XGBoost classifier with experiments tracked in MLflow, and a FastAPI service deployed on AWS. The harder lesson came from my uplift study on 64,000 customers. A campaign that clearly worked on average still harmed some customers, and a non-randomised list overstated its effect by 7.5×. I reported a failed placebo test openly and wrote the result as a two-page decision memo, a recommendation rather than a list of options.

I am comfortable with messy data and building the models I need. My forecasting platform uses dbt and DuckDB inside a Prefect pipeline, with tests that fail the build on leakage.

I also know the setting your product lives in: I hold the Claude Code in Action certificate and have built projects with AI coding tools. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Geneva. I would be glad to talk.
