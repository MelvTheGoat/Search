## Cover letter

Glean's search team invents new signals for personalisation, trains models over those signals, and combines LLMs with search to answer hard questions. Retrieval quality is the part of AI work I have measured most closely.

My compliance assistant uses hybrid retrieval: BM25 and dense MiniLM embeddings served with ONNX, fused with reciprocal rank fusion. On a 50-question labelled set it reaches 0.96 recall@5 and 0.842 MRR, with no misses in the top 10. I designed section-aware chunking so every citation maps to exactly one clause, and checked it against a fixed-window baseline instead of assuming it helped.

Ranking is also how I evaluated my fraud models: precision in the top 0.1%, 0.5% and 1% of scored transactions, because that is the list reviewers actually see. My Premier League predictor blends a LightGBM classifier over a 204-column feature table with a logistic regression, which is how I think about combining signals.

I have also applied for the Assistant Quality role and would welcome being considered for whichever team fits best. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Mountain View. I would be glad to talk.
