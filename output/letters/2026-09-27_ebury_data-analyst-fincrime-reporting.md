## Cover letter

Ebury's FinCrime data team builds the models, detection rules and reports behind transaction monitoring, customer risk scoring and investigations. Detection that must also stand up to an audit is exactly where my projects sit.

My fraud detection system scores transactions, breaks results down by attack type, card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise, and measures precision at fixed review capacity, the question an alert team actually faces. Every decision is logged in an append-only audit trail.

Reckon, my payment reconciliation system, joins processor webhooks with bank settlement files to find duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies, and gives reviewers a screen with match confidence, audit trails and suggested actions. My web3-risk-mcp tool screens crypto wallets for fund links to mixers and sanctioned addresses, which is screening logic in another setting.

I have also built a compliance assistant over central bank circulars and data protection law, so reading regulation and turning it into logic is familiar.

I use SQL, dbt, Python and Git, and I analysed financial records for irregularities as an internal auditor. I have not used Looker yet. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Madrid or Málaga. I would be glad to talk.

## Why this company

Ebury moves money across many currencies and jurisdictions, so financial crime controls have to scale with it. The team builds those controls as data products, curated models, detection rules and board reports, which suits someone who likes controls to be traceable.
