## Cover letter

White Circle wants ML engineers to turn very large volumes of unstructured text into topics, clusters and anomalies, with embedding and retrieval pipelines and careful evaluation of fuzzy problems. Retrieval and evaluation are where my strongest work is.

My compliance assistant embeds regulatory text with an ONNX-served MiniLM model and fuses dense and BM25 retrieval through reciprocal rank fusion, reaching 0.96 recall@5 and 0.842 MRR on a 50-question labelled golden set. Section-aware chunking keeps every citation tied to exactly one clause, and I tested it against a structure-blind baseline rather than assuming it was better. It also refuses rather than guesses, a small version of the policy checks your platform runs at scale.

For pipelines, my forecasting platform runs ingestion, dbt and DuckDB transformations and drift monitoring with Evidently, with tests that fail the build on leakage. My reconciliation system sends uncertain matches to a human review screen, the same pattern as human-in-the-loop labelling.

I have not worked at petabyte scale or measured inter-annotator agreement. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Paris or London. I would be glad to talk.
