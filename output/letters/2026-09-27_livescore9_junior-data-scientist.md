## Cover letter

LiveScore Group wants a junior data scientist to own its Marketing Mix Modelling pipeline and calibrate it with incrementality tests. Checking models against experimental ground truth is exactly what my strongest project is about.

I ran an uplift study on the Hillstrom randomised e-mail trial of 64,000 customers to decide who to contact under a fixed budget. It showed the campaign works, 1.25% conversion against 0.57%, but that a non-randomised list overstates the effect by 7.5×. That is the same reason MMM needs calibrating against tests. I added a power and MDE calculator and a CUPED variance reduction demo, and wrote the result up as a two-page decision memo for a marketing director.

For time series, my demand forecasting platform uses rolling-origin backtesting across 6 folds and ships the forecast that minimises business cost, not just error, which cut expected cost by 25%.

I also built a Premier League match predictor that retrains after every gameweek, so sports data is familiar ground.

I have a B.Sc. in Statistics, work in Python, pandas and SQL, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to London. I would be glad to talk.

## Why this company

LiveScore Group runs sports media and betting brands side by side, so marketing spend decisions are large and measurable. The role gives a junior hands-on ownership of the MMM pipeline, with incrementality tests to check it against, which is how I like to work.
