## Cover letter

This team turns vague ideas of what Claude can do into metrics that researchers and the public can rely on. I have spent most of my AI work on the same question at a much smaller scale: how do I know this system actually works?

For my compliance retrieval assistant, I built a 50-question labelled golden set and ran retrieval and generation as separate metrics, so a failure points to one component. Retrieval reached 0.96 recall@5 and 0.842 MRR. When refusal correctness came out at 0.40, I traced it to a lexical-overlap limit in the offline judge, not the model, and flagged live re-validation as the next step instead of reporting a number I did not trust. It is the model-or-infrastructure question your post describes, in miniature.

For web3-risk-mcp, an MCP server that gives Claude Desktop and Cursor six risk tools, I built a record and replay harness over 34 hand-checked addresses so results are repeatable.

My forecasting platform adds the infrastructure side: a scheduled pipeline with backtesting, drift monitoring and tests that fail the build on leakage. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics and have completed Claude Code in Action.

I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to San Francisco or New York. I would be glad to talk.
