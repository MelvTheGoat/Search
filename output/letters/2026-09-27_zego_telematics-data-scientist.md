## Cover letter

Zego's Telematics team turns raw phone sensor data into behavioural signals about how people drive, and those signals feed pricing. Building features from noisy event data, and testing whether they really predict something, is the work I enjoy most.

My fraud detection project models customer transaction histories as sequences. I compared a GRU, a temporal convolutional network and a causally masked Transformer against a LightGBM baseline built on velocity and deviation features, and reported results across 3 seeds so a lucky run could not decide the answer. Deep learning on sequential data, with a strong feature baseline beside it, is close to what telematics needs.

My demand forecasting platform shows the production side. It is an end-to-end pipeline in Python and SQL, orchestrated with Prefect over dbt and DuckDB, validated with rolling-origin backtesting across 6 folds, with leakage tests that fail the build. It runs, monitors and retrains itself rather than living in a notebook.

I have not worked with GPS or accelerometer signals yet, and I would like to learn signal processing from your senior team. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to London. I would be glad to talk.

## Why this company

Zego wants insurance priced on how people actually drive, which only works if the driving signals are right. The Telematics team builds those signals, sits between data science and engineering, and welcomes people eager to learn from senior peers.
