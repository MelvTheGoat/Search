## Cover letter

Ebury wants its lead scoring, conversion and profitability models built straight into the tools its sales teams use every day. I like that brief, because a model only matters once someone acts on it.

The closest work I have done is an uplift modelling study on 64,000 customers from a randomised e-mail trial. The question was not who will convert, but who converts because they were contacted. I built S-, T- and X-learners and a causal forest, checked them first on synthetic data where the true effect was known, and showed that a plain response model would mail five times as many customers the campaign harms. I wrote it up as a two-page memo for a marketing director, including a placebo test that failed, because a decision-maker needs to know that too.

I have also built a churn model with XGBoost, tracked in MLflow and served through FastAPI on AWS, and credit models chosen on calibration rather than ranking. My projects run on GCP Cloud Run, and I have built a retrieval system on top of an LLM.

I work in Python and SQL, and I would enjoy working next to senior data scientists and sales analysts to get models used. I can start immediately and would relocate to Madrid.
