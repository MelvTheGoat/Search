## Cover letter

This role supports model development, implementation and governance for financial crime risk analytics at FNB, covering AML/CFT, transaction monitoring and sanctions screening. Financial crime detection with clear documentation is the area my projects focus on.

My fraud detection system scores transaction sequences and reports precision at fixed review capacity, the question a monitoring team faces when alerts outnumber analysts. I broke results down by card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise, handled extreme class imbalance while keeping probabilities calibrated, and log every decision in an append-only audit trail.

For screening, my web3-risk-mcp tool traces fund links to mixers, sanctioned wallets, exploiters and phishing addresses over 1 or 2 hops, with an explainable score where every finding has a stable ID. For regulation, I built a compliance assistant over central bank circulars and data protection law, and my credit decisioning system keeps reason codes for each decision, which supports model governance.

I work in SQL and Python and have not used SAS yet, though I would pick it up quickly. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Johannesburg. I would be glad to talk.
