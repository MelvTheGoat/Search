## Cover letter

This role moves across pricing, fraud and behaviour detection, and agentic AI for Lyft Business rather than staying in one area. My projects cover those same three areas, so the breadth is what drew me.

On fraud, I built sequence models over customer transaction histories and compared them with a LightGBM baseline by attack type. They cut expected cost per transaction by 27% under an explicit cost model, and the served model runs at 5.1 ms p99 through ONNX Runtime.

On pricing and marketplace work, my demand forecasting platform predicts hourly taxi demand across NYC zones. Because unmet demand costs more than idle supply, I shipped the cost-optimal quantile, which cut expected cost by 25%. I also ran an uplift study on 64,000 customers showing that targeting by uplift, not response, avoids mailing customers the campaign harms.

On LLM systems, I built a retrieval assistant with guardrails and citation checks, and an MCP server that gives AI assistants tools to investigate crypto risk.

I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Toronto. I would be glad to talk.

Lyft Business serves companies, healthcare providers and travel programs through APIs, so its pricing and fraud models run on business rides with clear costs. The role takes prototypes to production across several ML problems, which suits someone who has built across fraud, forecasting and LLM systems.
