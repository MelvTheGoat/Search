## Cover letter

Notion is building one place where teams and AI agents work side by side, and this early career role covers AI product engineering, model systems and evals. I have built in all three areas on my own, and I would like to do it on a product millions of people use.

My compliance assistant is an AI feature built end to end. Hybrid retrieval, BM25 and dense embeddings fused with reciprocal rank fusion, reaches 0.96 recall@5. Generation has guardrails that make it refuse rather than guess, and a check strips any citation it never retrieved. It runs on GCP Cloud Run in about 225MB of memory, with request caps to bound cost.

For evals, I built its 50-question labelled set and measured retrieval and generation separately. For my MCP server, which gives AI assistants six tools to check crypto risk, I built a record and replay harness over 34 hand-checked addresses.

On the product side, I have built a React review interface and a PostgreSQL ledger API with 138 tests, so I am comfortable moving across UI, APIs and data.

I can start full time right away and am willing to relocate to San Francisco or New York. I would be glad to talk.
