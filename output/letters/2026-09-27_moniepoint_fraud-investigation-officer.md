## Cover letter

Moniepoint's fraud team investigates suspicious activity, finds fraud trends and recommends control improvements to cut losses. I have built fraud detection systems and investigated irregular records by hand, and this role brings both together.

As an internal auditor at NISER, I analysed financial and operational records to find inconsistencies and irregularities, and documented what I found so others could act on it. In my own projects I built the detection side: a fraud system that studies card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise separately, because each leaves a different pattern, and ranks transactions so an investigator sees the riskiest first.

For recommending controls, my web3-risk-mcp tool turns findings into an explainable score with a written reason for every point, and my reconciliation system flags duplicate settlements and payout gaps with an audit trail and suggested actions.

I also built a compliance assistant over central bank circulars and data protection law, so I know the regulation your investigations sit within. I can start immediately and work remotely. I would be glad to talk.

Oluwatobi Melvyn Mayungbo
mlvyn.t@gmail.com

## Why this company

Moniepoint serves millions of businesses and individuals, so fraud trends show up there early and at scale. The role mixes investigation, trend analysis and control design, and it can be done remotely.
