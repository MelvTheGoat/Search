## Cover letter

Synapse wants someone who can learn to translate a bank's credit and risk policy into Konan configurations, and explain the result to both technical and business people. I already know what a scorecard is, and I have built one into a working system.

My credit decisioning system turns policy into testable logic: calibrated default probabilities, approve and decline cutoffs set from an explicit cost model, adverse action reason codes for each decision, and an append-only audit trail. I compared a WOE-binned logistic scorecard with gradient boosting and documented why I chose one, the kind of validation your senior SME will expect.

In web3-risk-mcp, I wrote rule-based risk logic where each finding has a stable ID and a clear reason, and tested it against 34 hand-checked cases. That is close to checking that an implemented policy behaves as the client intended.

I teach machine learning to student cohorts, so explaining technical ideas to mixed audiences is part of my job, and I keep my project work documented and version-controlled on GitHub. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Cairo. I would be glad to talk.
