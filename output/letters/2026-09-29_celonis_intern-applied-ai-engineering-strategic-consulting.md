## Cover letter

Celonis' CoE Tiger Team turns client goals into technical solutions and reusable assets, and the post asks for strong Python for scripting, automation and LLM integrations. That is what I build.

I built and deployed a retrieval assistant over regulatory documents with hybrid search, a refusal guardrail when the sources do not support an answer, and a check that removes any citation the model never retrieved. I measured it on a 50-question golden set before trusting it, reaching 0.96 recall@5. I also built an MCP server that gives AI assistants six tools to check crypto risk, with caching, rate limits and retries so one failing source never breaks a result.

On the process side, I built a payment reconciliation pipeline with DuckDB and FastAPI that matches transaction feeds, and a forecasting pipeline that runs ingestion, dbt transformations, backtesting and retraining on its own with Prefect. Both are close to the process data and automation Celonis works with.

I teach machine learning to student cohorts, so explaining technical ideas to people who are not engineers is part of my week. I am available full time for at least 6 months, can start immediately, am willing to relocate to Madrid and would be glad to talk.
