## Cover letter

Quora's data team designs randomised experiments, finds the root causes of metric movements and shapes product strategy with evidence. Experiments and honest measurement are what I enjoy most in data science.

In my uplift study on the Hillstrom randomised e-mail trial of 64,000 customers, I showed the campaign worked, 1.25% conversion against 0.57%, but that a non-randomised list would have overstated the effect by 7.5×. I evaluated targeting with Qini curves, AUUC and bootstrap intervals, reported a failed placebo test openly, and wrote the result up as a two-page decision memo. I also built a power and MDE calculator and a CUPED variance reduction demo, the tools I would reach for when planning a product experiment.

For behaviour over time, my forecasting platform uses rolling-origin backtesting, and my churn model predicts which customers are likely to leave.

I work in SQL and Python, hold a B.Sc. in Statistics and teach machine learning. Your coordination hours fall in my afternoon and evening in UTC+1. I can start immediately and would be glad to talk.
