## Cover letter

Renmoney's fraud team needs an analyst to review transactions for red flags, support investigations with clean evidence, and help build monitoring that tracks fraud risks and control gaps. My audit work and my fraud projects cover each of these.

As an internal auditor, I analysed financial and operational records to find inconsistencies and irregularities, checking complex transaction data by hand. That taught me to verify every figure before it goes into a report.

My fraud detection system models customer transaction histories and breaks results down by card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise, because each fraud pattern looks different in the data. I measured precision in the top 0.1%, 0.5% and 1% of transactions, which is how a small team decides which alerts to investigate first.

I also built a reconciliation system that detects duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies, with a review screen showing audit trails and suggested actions, and an explainable risk scorer that gives a reason for every point. Clear reasons make an evidence file easier to defend.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, work in SQL and Python, and can start immediately. I would be glad to talk.

## Why this company

Renmoney lends to individuals and small businesses who are often left out by banks, so stopping fraud protects both customers and the loan book. The role is built for someone early in fraud work who wants to grow into it.
