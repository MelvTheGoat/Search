## Cover letter

Mastercard's Analytics and Automation team wants an AI Engineer to build generative AI solutions with RAG and agents, and to apply responsible AI practices such as bias detection, hallucination control and output guardrails. I have built both sides of that.

My compliance assistant combines BM25 and dense retrieval through reciprocal rank fusion and reached 0.96 recall@5 and 0.842 MRR on a 50-question labelled golden set. To control hallucination, it refuses rather than guesses and strips any citation the model gave but never retrieved. I deployed it on GCP Cloud Run with Docker, with request caps to bound cost.

For fairness, my credit decisioning system audits outcomes across sex, age, education and marital status using demographic parity and equal opportunity differences, and presents the accuracy and fairness trade-off as a policy decision. I have also deployed a model on AWS ECS Fargate with CI/CD, and built an MCP server that gives AI assistants read-only tools.

I have not used Azure, Databricks or LangChain. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Pune. I would be glad to talk.
