## Cover letter

NielsenIQ's Operations team is responsible for the quality of the data clients receive, and this role finds the root cause of data issues and keeps the classification models in shape. Root-cause work on data is something I have done both by hand and in code.

As an internal auditor at NISER, I went through financial and operational records to find inconsistencies and irregularities by hand, across complex transactional datasets. It taught me to check what a field really contains before trusting it.

In code, my payment reconciliation system matches records from different sources with temporal joins and tolerance thresholds, and flags duplicates and discrepancies for review. My demand forecasting platform retrains models when drift monitoring says so, tracks runs in MLflow, and has tests that fail the build when data leaks from the future. I have also built a churn classifier with XGBoost and tracked its experiments in MLflow.

I work in SQL and Python, and teach both data analysis and model building. I can start immediately, am willing to relocate to Sofia, and am comfortable working with a manager in Paris. I would be glad to talk.
