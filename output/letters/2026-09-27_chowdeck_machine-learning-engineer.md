## Cover letter

Chowdeck wants ML engineers for forecasting, logistics optimisation, ETA prediction, recommendations and fraud detection. Two of those, forecasting and fraud, are the systems I have built most carefully.

My demand forecasting platform predicts hourly demand across NYC taxi zones for the next 24 hours, a close cousin of delivery demand. It runs as a Prefect pipeline with dbt and DuckDB, backtesting, drift monitoring and conditional retraining. Because unmet demand costs more than idle supply, I shipped the cost-optimal quantile rather than the median, which cut expected cost by 25% and beat a seasonal-naive baseline by 32%.

For fraud, I compared sequence models with LightGBM by attack type and served the chosen model at 5.1 ms p99 through ONNX Runtime and FastAPI. For personalisation and experiments, my uplift study on 64,000 customers shows who to target and who a campaign harms, with a power and MDE calculator for planning tests.

I write tested Python and deploy with Docker and GitHub Actions. I can start immediately and work onsite with your team. I would be glad to talk.
