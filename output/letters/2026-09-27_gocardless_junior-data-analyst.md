## Cover letter

GoCardless moves bank payments for a very large number of businesses, and its analytics team helps every part of the company make faster, data-driven decisions. I want to start my data career on payments data, and this role is a direct way in.

Payments data is where I have spent most of my project time. Reckon, my payment reconciliation system, matches processor webhooks with bank settlement files to find uncollected revenue, duplicate settlements and payout differences. Getting there meant lining up data from different sources and choosing join rules and tolerance thresholds for records that arrive at different times, before any match could be trusted. I also built a review screen so people could act on the results without reading code.

For data models, I use dbt and DuckDB in my demand forecasting platform, with tests that stop bad data from flowing through.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics and the Google Data Analytics and Advanced Data Analytics certificates, and as a machine learning instructor I explain technical work to learners from different backgrounds. I have not used Looker or DOMO yet but would pick them up quickly. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Riga. I would be glad to talk.

GoCardless is a bank payments company, so its data covers the same flows I have modelled in my own projects, collections, settlements and fraud. The role is built for someone starting a data career, with dbt and BI tools at the centre and room to learn data engineering too.
