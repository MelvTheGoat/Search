## Cover letter

Oscar's Data team connects claims, clinical records and product data to manage risk and improve care, and this role builds the pipelines, models and validation checks behind that work. Validation is the part of data science I care about most.

My demand forecasting platform runs as a Prefect pipeline over dbt and DuckDB, from ingestion through transformation, backtesting, drift monitoring and retraining. I validated it with rolling-origin backtesting across 6 folds and wrote leakage tests that fail the build if any feature reads past the forecast origin. It beat a seasonal-naive baseline by 32%.

In my credit risk project, I chose between gradient boosting and a logistic scorecard on calibration, using Brier score and reliability diagrams, because the decisions downstream needed true probabilities. Every decision is kept in an append-only audit trail, the kind of standard a regulated industry like health insurance needs.

Before that, as an internal auditor at NISER, I analysed financial and operational records by hand to find inconsistencies and irregularities. It taught me not to trust data until it has been checked.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to New York, Los Angeles or Tempe. I would be glad to talk about the role.
