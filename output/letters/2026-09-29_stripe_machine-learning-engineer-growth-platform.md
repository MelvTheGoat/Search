## Cover letter

Stripe's Growth Platform decides which recommendation to show a business, when to show it and how to learn from the outcome, while avoiding unnecessary messages. Deciding who should receive an action, and who should not, is the problem at the centre of my uplift work.

I ran an uplift study on 64,000 customers with S, T and X learners and a causal forest. It showed that a response model mails five times as many customers the campaign harms as an uplift model does, which is the cost of showing a recommendation to the wrong people. Better targeting was worth about $1,390 against about $3,170 for a bigger budget, measured with Qini curves and bootstrap intervals.

I also own models end to end. My fraud system trains sequence models over transaction histories and serves them with ONNX Runtime and FastAPI at 5.1 ms p99. My forecasting platform runs ingestion, backtesting, drift monitoring and conditional retraining on its own, with experiments tracked in MLflow.

The post asks for more years than I have, so I am applying on the strength of this work. I can start immediately, am willing to relocate to San Francisco and would be glad to talk.

Stripe's recommendations reach millions of businesses, so a small gain in relevance helps many companies grow. The team uses contextual bandits and agents, which is where my causal work leads next.
