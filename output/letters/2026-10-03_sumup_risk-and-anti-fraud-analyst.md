## Cover letter

SumUp's Risk and Fraud team wants an analyst to monitor transactions and merchant activity, review fraud notifications from acquirers and investigate cases, with an interest in how rules and models flag them. My fraud projects and my audit work prepare me for this.

My fraud detection system models customer transaction histories and breaks results down by card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise. I measured precision in the top 0.1%, 0.5% and 1% of transactions, which is how a team decides which cases to review first.

I also built an explainable risk scorer that gives a reason for every point, and a payment reconciliation system that detects duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies, with a review screen showing audit trails. As an internal auditor, I checked complex transaction data by hand for irregularities, which taught me to check every figure before it goes into a report.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, work with data in SQL and Python, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Sofia. I would be glad to talk.
