## Cover letter

This role reviews fraud alerts, designs and tunes fraud rules, and monitors merchants for account takeover, friendly fraud and chargebacks. I have built detection systems from both sides, rules and models, and I would like to apply them to Flutterwave's merchants.

My fraud detection system studies transactions by attack type, card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise, and measures precision in the top 0.1%, 0.5% and 1% of scored transactions, because a review team can only look at so many alerts. Rule quality matters just as much. In web3-risk-mcp I designed an explainable rule-based scorer where every finding has a stable ID and a reason, and decisive findings cannot be hidden by other signals. That is how I would approach rule design and optimisation.

Reckon, my reconciliation system, flags duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies and gives reviewers a screen with match confidence and suggested actions, which is close to chargeback monitoring.

As an internal auditor at NISER, I reviewed financial records by hand for irregularities, so investigation work is familiar. I can start immediately and work onsite with your team. I would be glad to talk.

## Why this company

Flutterwave processes card and local payments for merchants in many markets, so fraud and brand risk decisions protect its card scheme standing as well as its customers. The role combines investigation with rule design, which suits someone who builds detection systems.
