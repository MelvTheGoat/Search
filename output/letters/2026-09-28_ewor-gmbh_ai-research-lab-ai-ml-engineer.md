## Cover letter

EWOR wants builders who take a new AI initiative from prototype to production with full ownership and no layers of approval. Every project I have shipped was built that way, alone and end to end.

My fraud detection system compares a GRU, a temporal convolutional network and a causally masked Transformer over transaction histories, each under 115k parameters. I solved a real trade-off between calibration and ranking, brought calibration error down to 0.0005, and served the model with ONNX Runtime and FastAPI at 5.1 ms p99 against a 50 ms budget.

On the language side, I built and deployed a retrieval assistant with hybrid search, a refusal guardrail and citation checks, and measured it on a 50-question golden set before trusting it. I also built an MCP server that gives AI assistants six tools to check crypto risk, with caching, rate limits and retries so one failing source never breaks a result.

Each of these went from an idea to a deployed service on GCP Cloud Run, Railway or AWS. I work in UTC+1, am open to remote work or relocating, can start immediately and would be glad to talk.
