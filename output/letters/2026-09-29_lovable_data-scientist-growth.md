## Cover letter

Lovable wants its first growth data scientist to find the lever in the funnel, run the experiment and act on the result, with strong SQL, Python and applied statistics. Experiments and the decisions behind them are what my strongest project is about.

I ran an uplift study on the Hillstrom e-mail trial with 64,000 customers to decide who to contact under a fixed budget. The campaign worked, with 1.25% conversion against 0.57%, but a non-randomised list overstated the effect by 7.5×, and a response model mailed five times as many customers the campaign harms as an uplift model did. Better targeting was worth about $1,390 against about $3,170 for a bigger budget, and I delivered it all as a two-page decision memo.

I also built a power and MDE calculator and a CUPED variance-reduction demo, so tests reach a clear answer sooner, and I know when a directional read is enough. The project has 135 tests and CI.

I use AI tools daily and have built LLM applications, including a retrieval assistant with its own evaluation. I can start immediately, am willing to relocate to London and would be glad to talk.
