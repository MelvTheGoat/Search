## Cover letter

RMB's Risk and Compliance team wants an operational risk analyst to monitor KRIs, support PRCIA reviews, log and track risk incidents, and prepare reports for the ORM and CRO. Careful monitoring and clean records are the parts of risk work I am strongest at.

As an internal auditor at NISER, I analysed financial and operational records to find inconsistencies and irregularities, and followed findings through until they were resolved. That is close to making sure every KRI breach has an action plan and every event log is approved on time.

In my own projects, I build controls into the system itself. My credit decisioning system keeps an append-only audit trail so any decision can be rebuilt months later. My reconciliation system flags duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies for review, with audit trails and suggested actions. I also built a compliance assistant over central bank circulars and data protection law, so regulatory reading is familiar.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, work in SQL and Python, and can turn risk data into clear reports. I can start immediately and work onsite with your team. I would be glad to talk.
