## Cover letter

Tower's ML research platform has to make large-scale experiments, backtests and model training transparent and reproducible for quantitative researchers. Reproducibility is something I have built into every project, even at small scale.

My demand forecasting platform tracks every run in MLflow, validates with rolling-origin backtesting across 6 expanding-window folds, and has automated leakage tests that fail the build if any feature reads past the forecast origin. My Premier League predictor is backtested gameweek by gameweek over 1,050 matches, with a public record of every past prediction.

For model work, I compared three deep sequence architectures in PyTorch with a LightGBM baseline across 3 seeds, then exported the chosen model to ONNX Runtime, 2.9× faster than eager PyTorch, and served it at 5.1 ms p99. For systems, I load tested my PostgreSQL ledger API to find where throughput saturates and why.

I have not run distributed training on an HPC cluster yet, and that is what I would most like to learn from your team. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Hong Kong. I would be glad to talk.
