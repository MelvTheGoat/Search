## Cover letter

Elliptic's Operations team wants an Agent Engineer to build agent fleets that automate core business processes, with evals that measure agent quality and APIs that connect AI to business systems. My work sits close to Elliptic's own mission of making crypto safer.

My web3-risk-mcp server gives AI assistants six read-only tools to check a crypto wallet, token or contract before anyone interacts with it, and returns a 0 to 100 risk score with a reason for every point. It detects honeypot tokens, rug pull signs and fund links to mixers, sanctioned wallets and phishing addresses over 1 or 2 hops. I measured it with a record and replay harness on 34 hand-checked addresses, reporting ROC AUC, precision and recall.

For evals, my compliance assistant scores retrieval and generation separately, so a failure can be traced to one component. My reconciliation system is deployed and sends exceptions to a review screen, so people stay in charge of the hard cases.

I have deployed on AWS ECS Fargate with CI/CD. I have not used LangChain or LangSmith, and I would learn them quickly. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to London. I would be glad to talk.
