## Cover letter

The Junior Data and ML Analyst role is about data people can trust: quality checks on client deliverables, SQL queries that watch for deviations, and better ML training data. It is the quiet part of machine learning, and it is the part I have learned to take most seriously.

In my demand forecasting platform, I wrote automated leakage tests that fail the build if any feature reads past the forecast origin, and I validated the model with rolling-origin backtesting across 6 folds. In Reckon, my payment reconciliation system, I used DuckDB with tolerance thresholds to catch duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies between feeds that never quite agree. For my RAG assistant, I measured retrieval against a 50-question labelled golden set, and traced one weak score to the offline judge rather than the system.

Before that I worked as an internal auditor at NISER, checking financial and operational records for inconsistencies by hand. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics and the Google Data Analytics and Advanced Data Analytics certificates, and I now teach data analysis and machine learning to student cohorts.

I can start immediately and would relocate to Sofia. I would be glad to talk.
