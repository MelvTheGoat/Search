## Cover letter

GR8 Tech needs someone who can look at a deposit, a withdrawal and a payment history and decide quickly whether money should move. Most of my recent work sits inside exactly that kind of data.

I built Reckon, a payment reconciliation system that matches processor webhooks against bank settlement files and flags uncollected revenue, duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies. Reviewers see match confidence scores, an audit trail and suggested actions, so each exception is handled and documented the same way. I also built a fraud detection system over customer transaction histories and studied its results by attack type, including card testing and account takeover. For crypto payment rails, my web3-risk-mcp server checks wallets and tokens for mixer links, sanctioned addresses and phishing activity before anyone interacts with them.

Before that, as an internal auditor at NISER, I reviewed financial records for irregularities by hand.

I work in SQL and Python, which makes fast pattern searches easy, and I work in UTC+1. I can start immediately and would welcome a call about the role.

## Why this company

GR8 Tech runs payments for platforms with millions of active players, so fraud and payment errors show up fast and at scale. This role sits right on those money flows and feeds what it learns back into the risk tools. I want to work where each decision has a clear cost and a clear owner.
