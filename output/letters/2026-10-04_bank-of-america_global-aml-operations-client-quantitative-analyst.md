## Cover letter

Bank of America's Global AML Operations team wants a Client Quantitative Analyst to apply quantitative methods that support its BSA/AML compliance programs, and to explain those methods clearly to the business, risk managers and technology. My audit, fraud and reconciliation work are close to this.

As an internal auditor, I analysed financial and operational records to find inconsistencies and irregularities, doing manual anomaly detection across complex transaction data. I then built the same kind of checks in software. My fraud detection system models customer transaction histories and reports precision at fixed review capacity in the top 0.1%, 0.5% and 1% of transactions, with a breakdown by card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise. My reconciliation system finds duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies across multi-source transaction feeds.

I also built a credit decisioning system with reason codes and an append-only audit trail, so any decision can be reconstructed months later, which matters when someone asks why a decision was made.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, work in SQL and Python, can start immediately and am willing to relocate. I would be glad to talk.

AML analytics has to keep changing as risks and client behaviour change, and every method has to be documented for compliance. That mix of careful statistics and clear explanation is what I enjoy.
