## Cover letter

ElevenLabs wants a data scientist to own the safety datasets and evaluation workflows behind its AI Safety work, and to turn policy into clear labelling rules. Building evaluation sets people can trust is the part of ML work I take the most care over.

For my compliance retrieval assistant, I built a 50-question labelled golden set and measured retrieval and generation separately, so a failure points to one component. When a refusal score came out at 0.40, I traced it to a weakness in the offline judge rather than the model, and reported it that way instead of overstating readiness.

For web3-risk-mcp, a crypto risk tool for AI assistants, I assembled 34 hand-checked addresses, 12 risky and 22 safe, with a record and replay harness that reports ROC AUC, precision and recall. Every rule has a stable ID and a written reason, which is close to turning a policy into consistent decisions.

My fraud detection work looks at abuse from the other side: models judged per attack type, such as card testing and account takeover, and a human review screen in my reconciliation project.

I work in Python and SQL, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to London. I would be glad to talk.

## Why this company

ElevenLabs ships voice, music and agent products to millions of users, so safety has to be measured on real product data, not only in the lab. The role sits between data science, policy and engineering and starts hands-on with collection and labelling, which suits how I work.
