## Cover letter

ElevenLabs wants an Applied AI Engineer to build agents that run real parts of the business, made reliable, measurable and safe enough to depend on, working with its growth teams. Measuring whether an AI system really works is how I build.

My compliance assistant reached 0.96 recall@5 and 0.842 MRR on a 50-question labelled golden set. I scored retrieval and generation separately, and when the offline judge gave a 0.40 refusal-correctness result, I traced it to a limit in the judge itself, which is the kind of failure you only find by evaluating carefully. It refuses rather than guesses, and it is deployed on GCP Cloud Run with request caps.

My web3-risk-mcp server gives AI assistants six read-only tools with caching, rate limits and retries, measured on 34 hand-checked addresses. For growth, my uplift study on a randomised trial of 64,000 customers showed that a non-randomised list overstates a campaign's effect by 7.5×, which is why I always ask whether a result is real.

I work in Python and SQL with LLM APIs. I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and work in UTC+1. I would be glad to talk.
