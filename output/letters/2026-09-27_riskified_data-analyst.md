## Cover letter

Riskified's analysts work with enterprise merchants on their fraud performance in real time and research new online fraud trends. I want to do that research on live data, and my projects have prepared me for it.

My fraud detection system studies fraud by attack type, card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise, because each one looks different in the data. I measured models on precision at fixed review capacity, the top 0.1%, 0.5% and 1% of transactions, since that is what a merchant's team can actually review, and the sequence models cut expected cost per transaction by 27% under an explicit cost model.

For explaining results, my web3-risk-mcp tool gives a reason for every point of its risk score, and my reconciliation system shows reviewers match confidence and suggested actions. I teach machine learning to student cohorts, so I am used to explaining technical work to people without a technical background.

I work in SQL and Python. I have not used R at work, but the analysis carries over. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Lisbon. I would be glad to talk.

Oluwatobi Melvyn Mayungbo
mlvyn.t@gmail.com

## Why this company

Riskified sees fraud across many large merchants at once, so new trends show up there early. The analyst role is client-facing and research-heavy, which fits someone who likes both finding patterns and explaining them.
