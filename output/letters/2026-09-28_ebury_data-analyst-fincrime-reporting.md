## Cover letter

Ebury's FinCrime reporting team develops detection logic and monitoring rules, runs data quality checks, and supports audits with traceable data. My projects and my audit work cover the same ground.

I built web3-risk-mcp, a rule-based and fully explainable risk scorer. Every finding has a stable ID and a reason, decisive findings set a floor so trust signals can never hide them, and it traces fund links to mixers, sanctioned wallets and phishing addresses. That is monitoring logic an auditor can follow.

For transaction monitoring, my fraud detection system benchmarks sequence models against a LightGBM baseline on velocity and deviation features, and breaks results down by card testing, account takeover and merchant compromise. I also built a payment reconciliation pipeline with DuckDB that detects duplicate settlements and payout discrepancies, and a forecasting pipeline built on dbt.

Before this, as an internal auditor, I reviewed financial and operational records to find inconsistencies and irregularities. I write SQL and Python, hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate to Málaga or Madrid. I would be glad to talk.

Ebury moves money across borders for businesses, so good FinCrime data protects both clients and the firm's licences. The team uses dbt, BigQuery and Looker, and the role joins detection rules with regulatory reporting.
