## Cover letter

Block wants an engineer to build the evaluation platform for its AI products: graders that mix ground truth checks, rubrics and LLM-as-judge, and leaderboards that report confidence intervals so teams can tell real gains from noise. Evaluation design is where most of my AI work sits.

In my compliance retrieval assistant, I scored retrieval and generation as separate metrics so a failure could be traced to one component, reaching 0.96 recall@5 and 0.842 MRR on a 50-question labelled golden set. When the offline stub judge gave a 0.40 refusal-correctness result, I traced it to a lexical-overlap limit in the judge itself rather than reporting it as system behaviour, and named re-validation against a live provider as the next step. That is the judge calibration problem your post describes.

My statistics work covers the rest. My fraud system reports results across 3 seeds, my uplift study uses bootstrap intervals and a placebo test, and I built a power and MDE calculator with a CUPED demo. My forecasting platform runs as an orchestrated Prefect pipeline with tests that fail the build on leakage, and I ship services with Docker and GitHub Actions.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, can start immediately and am willing to relocate. I would be glad to talk.

Block treats evaluation as the ceiling on how good its AI products can be, and this role builds that platform early. Making evaluation statistically honest is the part of AI work I care about most.
