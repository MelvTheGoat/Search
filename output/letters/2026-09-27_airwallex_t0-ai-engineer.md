## Cover letter

Airwallex's AI team is automating payments, payroll, bookkeeping and tax for startups, and picks the right technique for each problem, from prompting to retrieval to fine-tuning. I build the same way, and finance is the setting I know best.

For agents and tool use, I built web3-risk-mcp, an MCP server that gives AI assistants six tools to check crypto wallets, tokens and contracts. Its HTTP layer caches, rate-limits and retries with backoff, so one failing source never breaks an investigation, and every score comes with reasons.

For retrieval, my compliance assistant combines BM25 and dense search to reach 0.96 recall@5, refuses rather than guesses, and strips citations it never retrieved. I evaluate it by component so I know what to fix.

On the finance side, Reckon, my payment reconciliation system, matches processor webhooks with bank settlement files and routes exceptions to a human review screen, the kind of bookkeeping work automation can take off a founder's plate. I have also built a credit decisioning system with reason codes and an audit trail, so compliance is part of how I design.

I have not fine-tuned models in production. I can start immediately and am willing to relocate to San Francisco. I would be glad to talk.
