## Cover letter

Experian's Commercial Bureau Analytics team needs an analyst to build bureau-based credit risk scorecards, validate them on calibration and out-of-time tests, and explain the results to lenders. Scorecards and calibration are the centre of my credit work.

I built a credit decisioning system that produces calibrated default probabilities, cost-optimal approve and decline cutoffs and adverse action reason codes, with an audit trail that lets any decision be rebuilt months later. I benchmarked gradient boosting against a WOE-binned logistic scorecard and chose on calibration, using Brier score, reliability diagrams and expected calibration error, because expected loss needs true probabilities. I also audited fairness across customer groups and presented the trade-off as a policy decision.

For validation, my forecasting platform uses rolling-origin backtesting across 6 folds and tests that fail the build if any feature reads past the forecast origin. My fraud project handled extreme class imbalance and reduced ECE to 0.0005 with temperature scaling and isotonic regression.

I hold a B.Sc. in Statistics, write Python and SQL, and teach machine learning, so I can explain methods to non-specialists. I have not worked with MSME bureau data and have fewer years than the post asks for, so I am applying on the strength of this work. I can start immediately and would be glad to talk.

## Why this company

Experian's bureau data sits behind lending decisions for banks and fintechs, so a sound scorecard there helps many lenders at once. Building and validating scorecards is the credit work I most want to do.
